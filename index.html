FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Komoka homeowners ask us most.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service in Komoka?

Yes. We offer emergency heating and cooling service across Komoka, Kilworth, and Ilderton, with technicians dispatched from London so response times stay short for the subdivisions west of the city.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are a fully licensed and insured HVAC company serving Komoka and the surrounding Middlesex County area, with certified technicians who work on all major brands.

My home is a newer build. Can you service its furnace and AC?

Absolutely. Most Komoka and Kilworth homes run high-efficiency gas furnaces and central air, and our technicians know these systems inside out. We carry the common parts and diagnose them quickly.

Do you install cold-climate heat pumps?

Yes. We install and service cold-climate heat pumps for homeowners looking to lower their bills, and newer Komoka builds are often already set up to make the switch straightforward.

Can you set up a smart thermostat in my new home?

Yes. We install smart and programmable thermostats and pair them properly with your furnace, heat pump, or multi-stage system, then walk you through the app and schedules.

How much does a service call cost?

We offer free, no-obligation quotes. Diagnosing the issue is straightforward, and we explain all pricing clearly before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Do you offer financing on new equipment?

Yes, we offer financing options on installations and major equipment like heat pumps and mini-splits. Ask us about current plans when you request your free quote.

Do you serve areas outside of Komoka?

Yes. We serve Komoka and nearby communities including Kilworth, Ilderton, London, Mount Brydges, and Delaware, plus the rural properties in between.
